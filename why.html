why it forgets

pensieve deletes your notes on purpose. write something and it goes lighter over the following days, and by default it is gone in a week. there is no archive and no search, so there is nowhere to go back to. i built it that way deliberately, and this page is why.

the thing i wanted to fix is that a notes app fills up and then it is a second inbox. not because any one note is a problem, but because the pile asks something of you every time you open it: sort me, file me, decide about me. and you never do, so it sits there getting bigger.

so this one does the deciding. a note fades, then it is gone, and you did not have to do anything. pinning exists for the few things that need longer, and pinning is a pause rather than a decision: the note stops fading, and when you unpin it it carries on from where it was.

a week is the default. or rather, a week is a guess that turned out to be about right, which is a weaker reason than it sounds like. it is in settings if you disagree.

the honest part: you will lose something you wanted to keep. that is not a bug i am planning to fix. it is the same mechanism as the part that works, and if that trade sounds bad then this is probably not the app for you, which is completely fine.

it is free. no account, nothing to sign up for, and no analytics of any kind, which also means i have no idea how many people use it or what they write. i would rather not know.

nguyen the anh
